- Made worker result artifact strings, check objects, and permitted status
  values explicit; the template now shows an unavailable required check.

  distinction between worker deliverables and pending lead acceptance.

  source-based pilot: Sol High authored, Luna Max reviewed, and the original

  validator/runtime changes. Installation qualification remains a separate
  check; see `docs/validation.md` for dated evidence.
