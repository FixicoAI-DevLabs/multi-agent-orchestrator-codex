# Acceptance review contract

The original lead normally performs final acceptance. Check artifacts and test
evidence yourself; agreement between models and a worker's completion statement
are not substitutes.

1. Match the incoming run, plan, assignment, attempt, task, and host identifiers.
2. Inspect actual delivered files against owned paths, source baseline, and each
   acceptance criterion. Verify relevant reported checks with available tools.
3. Check dependencies, permitted actions, unresolved questions, and integration.
4. Record `accepted`, `changes_requested`, or `rejected`, with evidence and reason.
   Keep `open` when evidence is insufficient. A worker's `needs_attention`,
   failed required check, missing artifact, or unmet criterion cannot pass.
5. For a correction, name the unmet criterion and expected evidence. Continue
   under existing unchanged approval only when its scope and limits cover that
   correction. Replacement/promotion consumes the same logical assignment's
   budget; a model/contract change requires appropriate new approval.

Close the run only after every required assignment is accepted and the final
integrated result has an accepted review. Resolve any remaining active or
uncertain worker, including optional work, before claiming completion. Report
partial/blocked results honestly; dropping a required deliverable requires an
explicit scope change, not an edited success label.

Review evidence paths are references, not proof by existence. The validator can
check their shape and relationship; the lead must establish their content and
relevance. Record unavailable cost data as unavailable, not zero.
